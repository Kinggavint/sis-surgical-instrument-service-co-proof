More Than 50 Years of Surgical Repair

SIS Has Grown with the Healthcare Industry Since 1971

SIS began as a specialist in surgical instrument repair and has expanded its capabilities over five decades to meet the evolving needs of hospitals and surgery centers. Today SIS covers surgical instruments, endoscopes, power equipment, and video systems, and operates Instrument Care Units inside healthcare facilities across the country.

  1. 1971

    SIS is founded in Glendale Heights, IL by a veteran and former OR nurse with a focus on surgical instrument repair for healthcare facilities.

  2. Expansion into Endoscope Repair

    As endoscopic procedures became standard in ORs across the country, SIS developed the technical expertise to repair both flexible and rigid endoscopes.

  3. Power Equipment and Video Systems

    SIS expanded its repair capabilities to include powered surgical tools and the video systems used in minimally invasive surgery, giving facilities a single repair partner for all OR equipment categories.

  4. Onsite Instrument Care Units

    SIS developed the Instrument Care Unit model, embedding technicians directly inside healthcare facilities to reduce turnaround times and provide on-the-floor repair support.

  5. Today

    SIS serves hospitals, surgery centers, and healthcare facilities nationwide from its headquarters in Glendale Heights, IL. Every repair still carries a one-year warranty, and SIS remains an independent third-party provider with no financial incentive to replace repairable equipment.

Why Independence Matters

SIS Has No Reason to Write Off Your Equipment

Original equipment manufacturers have a built-in incentive to declare aging instruments and scopes beyond repair and sell replacements. SIS does not sell new equipment. Our business depends entirely on our ability to repair and restore your instruments, scopes, and OR equipment successfully. That independence means we approach every item with the goal of returning it to service. If an item can be repaired, SIS will repair it. We disclose our assessment honestly so your team can make informed decisions. Our one-year warranty on all repairs reflects our confidence in the work we do.

Our Commitments to Your Facility

What SIS Delivers on Every Repair Contract

SIS structures every client relationship around four commitments that have guided the company since 1971.

Honest, Independent Assessment

SIS evaluates each item on its actual repair potential. We provide a written assessment before any work begins so your team knows exactly what is being repaired and why.

Significant Cost Reduction

Facilities that work with SIS typically reduce repair costs by 30 to 70 percent compared to OEM repair pricing. That saving applies across instruments, scopes, power equipment, and video systems.

One-Year Warranty on All Work

Every repair SIS performs is covered by a one-year warranty. If a repaired item fails within that period due to a workmanship issue, SIS addresses it at no additional cost.

National Reach, Onsite Presence

SIS serves healthcare facilities nationwide and can place Instrument Care Unit technicians directly in your facility to reduce shipping time and instrument shortages.
