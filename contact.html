Tell Us About Your Facility and What You Need Repaired

Whether you manage a single surgery center or a multi-facility health system, SIS can structure a repair relationship that fits your volume and equipment categories. Provide your contact information and a brief description of your repair needs, and a SIS representative will be in touch.

Submit Your Inquiry

What to Expect After You Contact SIS

A Straightforward Path from First Contact to Active Repair Partnership

SIS keeps the onboarding process simple so your facility can start benefiting from lower repair costs and a single-partner service model as quickly as possible.

A SIS Representative Responds

After you submit your inquiry, a SIS representative will contact you to discuss your facility type, equipment categories, repair volumes, and current vendor arrangements.

Initial Repair Evaluation

SIS can begin with an evaluation of a specific instrument or a batch of equipment so you can see the repair quality and cost savings firsthand before committing to a contract.

Service Contract Discussion

If your facility is ready to consolidate repair vendors, SIS will discuss contract structures that cover all applicable equipment categories under a single service agreement.

Onsite Instrument Care Unit Option

For facilities with sufficient instrument volume, SIS can discuss placing a dedicated technician onsite to handle repairs and maintenance without shipping delays.
