What SIS Repairs

Detailed Repair Capabilities Across Four Equipment Categories

SIS technicians are trained and experienced across the equipment types most critical to surgical operations. The sections below describe what each service category covers and how SIS approaches the repair process.

Surgical Instrument Repair

SIS repairs general and specialty surgical instruments including scissors, forceps, needle holders, clamps, retractors, and more. Technicians assess each instrument for alignment, tension, sharpness, and surface integrity. Instruments that can be restored are returned to full clinical function. SIS does not automatically write off instruments that other vendors might declare unrepairable.

Endoscope Repair

SIS repairs both flexible and rigid endoscopes. Flexible scope repairs address angulation control, optical clarity, insertion tube integrity, and leak testing. Rigid scope repairs address rod lens systems, light transmission, and mechanical housing. Scopes are tested before and after repair to confirm they meet performance standards.

Power Equipment Repair

Surgical drills, saws, shavers, and their attachments are subject to frequent sterilization cycles and demanding use. SIS services and repairs powered surgical equipment, replacing worn components and verifying torque, speed, and safety function before returning equipment to the facility.

Video System Repair

SIS repairs the video equipment used in laparoscopic, arthroscopic, and other minimally invasive procedures. This includes surgical cameras and camera heads, light sources, monitors, insufflators, and video integration components. Functional video systems are essential to safe minimally invasive surgery, and SIS treats these repairs with the same priority as instrument repairs.

Onsite Instrument Care Units

SIS operates Instrument Care Units embedded directly inside healthcare facilities. An onsite SIS technician handles routine maintenance, sharpenings, and urgent repairs without the facility needing to ship equipment off-site. This reduces turnaround time, minimizes instrument shortages, and gives OR staff a direct point of contact for repair needs.

Repair Contracts and Service Agreements

SIS offers multi-instrument, multi-category repair contracts that consolidate what many facilities manage across several OEM vendor relationships. A single SIS agreement covers all applicable equipment categories, simplifies invoicing, and provides predictable repair costs. SIS works with each facility to structure an agreement that fits its OR volume and equipment inventory.

How a SIS Repair Works

From Evaluation to Return to Service in Clear Steps

SIS follows a consistent repair process designed to give facilities transparency and confidence at every stage.

  1. Submit Equipment for Evaluation

    Send your instruments or equipment to SIS or request an onsite evaluation through your Instrument Care Unit. SIS accepts both individual items and batches.

  2. Receive a Repair Assessment

    SIS technicians inspect each item and document its condition. You receive a clear assessment of what can be repaired, what the repair involves, and the estimated cost before any work begins.

  3. Approve and Authorize Repair

    Your facility reviews the assessment and approves the repair. SIS does not begin work without authorization, giving you full control over repair decisions and spending.

  4. Repair and Quality Check

    SIS technicians complete the repair and run post-repair quality checks specific to the equipment type, whether that means leak testing a scope, verifying instrument tension, or confirming power tool torque.

  5. Return to Service with a One-Year Warranty

    Repaired equipment is returned to your facility ready for clinical use. Every repair is covered by a one-year warranty. If an issue arises, SIS addresses it.
