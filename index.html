What Sets SIS Apart

One Independent Partner Instead of Many OEM Contracts

Managing multiple original equipment manufacturer contracts is costly and time-consuming. SIS covers surgical instruments, endoscopes, power equipment, and video systems under a single service agreement, giving your facility one point of contact and one consistent standard of repair.

No Conflict of Interest

As an independent third-party provider, SIS has no financial reason to declare an item beyond repair. Our goal is to restore your equipment, not sell you a replacement.

Faster Turnaround with Onsite Units

SIS operates Instrument Care Units inside healthcare facilities across the country. Onsite service means shorter instrument downtime and fewer disruptions to your surgical schedule.

Every Repair Backed by a One-Year Warranty

SIS stands behind its work. Every repair we complete carries a one-year warranty so your team can return equipment to service with confidence.
